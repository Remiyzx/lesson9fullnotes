Grammar Rules & Common Pitfalls

Rule A: Expressing Dates (BIG to SMALL) (Pg 141)

Always arrange time units from largest to smallest: Year -> Month -> Day -> Day of Week -> Time of Day.

✔ 2026 nián, shí yuè, èrshíqī hào, xīngqīsì, shàngwǔ
✘ Thursday morning, October 27th, 2026 (Never use English word order!)
Rule B: Time Word Position in Sentences (Pg 142)

Time words (e.g., Jīntiān, Míngtiān shàngwǔ) must go BEFORE the verb. They can go either right before or right after the Subject.

✔ Wǒ jīntiān hěn máng. (Subject + Time + Predicate)
✔ Míngtiān shàngwǔ nǐ yǒu kè ma? (Time + Subject + Predicate)
✘ Wǒ hěn máng jīntiān. (Never put time at the very end!)
Rule C: Telling Age (Nominal Predicate) (Pg 143)

Age is stated using a Nominal Predicate. DO NOT use the verb "shì" when telling age!

✔ Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.
✘ Tā jīnnián shì èrshí suì. (Do not use "shì" for age!)
Rule D: Making Suggestions with "... , hǎo ma?" (Pg 143)

Attach hǎo ma? to the end of a statement to make a polite proposal.

e.g., Wǒmen qù chī kǎoyā, hǎo ma? (We go eat roast duck, okay?)
Accepting: Hǎo a! / Tài hǎo le! / Hǎo.
Rule E: Future Repetition with "zài" (Pg 135)

Place zài BEFORE a verb to express repeating an action in the future.

Structure: zài + Verb
e.g., Shíyī yuè 12 hào wǒmen zài lái chī shòumiàn. (We will come again to eat noodles on Nov 12th.)

Complete Text Dialogues

Text 1: Making an Appointment & Asking Age (Pg 130-131)
1. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Lín Nà, nǐ zěnmeyàng?(Lin Na, how are you?)
2. Lín Nà: Wǒ jīntiān hěn máng.(I am very busy today.)
3. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Míngtiān shàngwǔ nǐ yǒu méiyǒu kè?(Do you have class tomorrow morning?)
4. Lín Nà: Míngtiān shì xīngqī jǐ?(What day of the week is tomorrow?)
5. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Míngtiān shì xīngqīsì.(Tomorrow is Thursday.)
6. Lín Nà: Wǒ shàngwǔ, xiàwǔ dōu yǒu kè.(I have classes both morning and afternoon.)
7. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Nǐ xīngqīrì yǒu shíjiān ma?(Do you have time on Sunday?)
8. Lín Nà: Xīngqīrì shì jǐ hào?(What date is Sunday?)
9. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Xīngqīrì shì shí yuè èrshíqī hào, shì Sòng Huá de shēngri.(Sunday is October 27th, it is Song Hua's birthday.)
10. Lín Nà: Shì ma? Tā jīnnián duō dà?(Really? How old is he this year?)
11. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Sòng Huá 1982 nián shí yuè èrshíqī rì chūshēng, shǔ gǒu. Tā jīnnián èrshí suì.(Song Hua was born on Oct 27, 1982, in the year of the dog. He is 20.)
12. Lín Nà: Tā shì nǎr rén?(Where is he from?)
13. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Tā shì Běijīng rén. Tā bàba, māma dōu zài Běijīng. Xīngqīrì xiàwǔ wǒmen yǒu yí ge jùhuì, zhùhè tā de shēngri. Lìbō, Dàwéi dōu qù, nǐ cānjiā bu cānjiā?(He is from Beijing. His dad and mom are both in Beijing. Sunday afternoon we have a party to celebrate his birthday. Libo and Dawei are both going, are you participating?)
14. Lín Nà: Tài hǎo le! Wǒ dāngrán cānjiā. Zhōngguó rén guò shēngri de shíhou chī dàngāo ma?(Great! Of course I will participate. Do Chinese people eat cake when celebrating birthdays?)
15. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Chī dàngāo.(Yes, eat cake.)
16. Lín Nà: Wǒ mǎi yí ge dà dàngāo, hǎo ma?(I'll buy a big cake, okay?)
17. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Hǎo a. Wǒ mǎi liǎng píng hóng pútaojiǔ.(Okay. I'll buy two bottles of red wine.)
Text 2: Celebrating the Birthday Party (Pg 134-135)
1. Lín Nà: Sòng Huá, zhè shì shēngri dàngāo. Zhù nǐ shēngri kuàilè!(Song Hua, this is the birthday cake. Happy birthday!)
2. Sòng Huá: Xièxie. Dàngāo zhēn piàoliang. Nǐmen lái, wǒ hěn gāoxìng.(Thank you. The cake is really beautiful. You all coming makes me very happy.)
3. Mǎ Dàwéi: Jīntiān wǒmen chī Běijīng kǎoyā. Wǒ hěn xǐhuan chī kǎoyā.(Today we eat Beijing roast duck. I really like eating roast duck.)
4. Dīng Lìbō: Wǒmen hē shénme jiǔ?(What wine are we drinking?)
5. Wáng Xiǎoyún: Dāngrán hē hóng pútaojiǔ, wǒmen hái chī shòumiàn.(Of course we drink red wine, we also eat longevity noodles.)
6. Lín Nà: Chī shòumiàn? Zhēn yǒu yìsi.(Eat longevity noodles? Really interesting.)
7. Sòng Huá: Lín Nà, nǐ de shēngri shì nǎ tiān?(Lin Na, which day is your birthday?)
8. Lín Nà: Shíyī yuè shí'èr hào.(November 12th.)
9. Sòng Huá: Hǎo, shíyī yuè shí'èr hào wǒmen zài lái chī shòumiàn.(Okay, on Nov 12th we will come again to eat longevity noodles.)

3. Answer the Questions (Pg 139)
  1. Yì nián yǒu jǐ ge yuè? (How many months are there in a year?)
    Yì nián yǒu shí'èr ge yuè. (There are 12 months in a year.)
  2. Yì nián yǒu duōshao ge xīngqī? (How many weeks are there in a year?)
    Yì nián yǒu wǔshí'èr ge xīngqī. (There are 52 weeks in a year.)
  3. Yí ge xīngqī yǒu jǐ tiān? (How many days are there in a week?)
    Yí ge xīngqī yǒu qī tiān. (There are 7 days in a week.)
  4. Shíyī yuè yǒu duōshao tiān? (How many days are there in November?)
    Shíyī yuè yǒu sānshí tiān. (There are 30 days in November.)
  5. Jīntiān jǐ yuè jǐ hào? Xīngqī jǐ? (What is today's date? What day of the week is it?)
    Jīntiān [Month] yuè [Day] hào, xīngqī [Weekday]. (Today is [Month] [Day], [Weekday].)
